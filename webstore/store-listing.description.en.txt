Vuora brings 13 everyday browsing aids together in one popup. Twelve of them can be switched on and off individually, and all twelve start OFF, so only the features you choose ever touch a page. The Color Picker is always available.

■ Main features

・📺 YouTube Enhancements (OFF by default)
  40 sub-features, each with its own switch.
  Video filters: hide playlists / mixes / individual Shorts / Shorts shelves / live, premiere and station items / members-only videos / already-watched videos / recommendation sections on Home. A channel blocklist removes chosen channels across YouTube, and you can hide "Most relevant" in Subscriptions and videos from channels outside your country.
  Search results: hide video shelves, card lists, courses, channel intro cards, Shorts carousels, related-search blocks and the Shorts filter chip; hide verified-channel, artist-channel or chapter videos; grey out results that don't contain your search terms; outline thumbnails; show results as a grid.
  Watch page: hide comments and live chat, show the original broadcast time on stream archives, live latency control (choose how to catch up and which live stats to show), quality / playback speed (0.25x–2x) / player volume selectors, and redirect Shorts URLs to the regular watch page. A "Reverse" button in the playlist panel plays playlists saved backwards (Part 10 → Part 1) from the last video forward, including next, previous and autoplay. For streams that show the game in part of the frame, drag over the game screen to zoom the player to just that area (saved per channel on your device).
  Menus & layout: hide the Shorts sidebar entry, show all subscriptions in the left menu, add an "All subscriptions" shortcut, turn the subscriptions page into a searchable grid, and lay out Home and feeds as an even grid (with selectable column count).
  Integrations: add the current video, search results, playlists or channel videos as sources to Gemini Notebook in your own Google account.
  Quality, speed, volume, live stats, game-screen zoom and broadcast time sit in one row of compact chips just above the control bar, so they barely cover the video. Everything else opens from a gear button and can be changed during playback. You can assign keyboard shortcuts for quality, speed and volume in your browser's extension shortcut settings.

・🐦 X Cleaner (OFF by default)
  10 sub-features to calm down X (formerly Twitter).
  Layout: hide the right pane (the timeline widens into the freed space) / trends / who to follow / the messages dock
  Noise: hide promoted posts / Premium upsells / Grok / engagement counts (replies, reposts, likes, views)
  Timeline: open Home on the "Following" tab
  Extras: hover a post image to show a save button and download it in original quality

・📷 Instagram Cleaner (OFF by default)
  12 sub-features to trim clutter and add video controls.
  Main: remove Reels (including URL redirects) / remove Explore / hide the Stories tray on Home / send Stories URLs back to Home / hide Threads promotion
  Extras: hide like and follower counts / stop videos in posts from autoplaying / hide comments / hide Notes / hide the unread DM badge / add a seek bar to videos (Space toggles playback while enabled) / add a save button to post photos

・🎵 TikTok Cleaner (OFF by default)
  3 sub-features: hide comments / hide suggested accounts / add a save button to post images.

・📖 Wikipedia reading settings (OFF by default)
  Rebuilds Wikipedia articles around search, contents, the article and its info box, and lets you adjust colors, body and heading typefaces, text size, line spacing, article width, link style and how much of the article to show (full / text and images / text only). Seven Japanese typefaces are bundled, so no fonts are downloaded from outside.
  Sub-features: modern article layout / sticky contents / image viewer / link and reference previews / hide the Appearance panel / keep the article within a set width
  Article text, editing links and attribution stay in place, and nothing is redirected or fetched from elsewhere. Turning it off restores the original page.

・📦 Amazon Subscribe & Save monthly total (OFF by default)
  Shows a per-month total (unit price × quantity) on the Subscribe & Save management page of amazon.co.jp. When a price isn't listed, the current Subscribe & Save price is retrieved from Amazon, falling back to the previous delivery's unit price for a reference total. Billed amounts may change and coupons aren't included. No partial total is shown unless every item's price is available.

・🏆 Amazon jump-to-ranking button (OFF by default)
  Bestseller rank links sit in a different spot on every product page. This gathers them into one button at the top of the product info that takes you to the most specific subcategory ranking.

・📦 Amazon seller / shipper badge (OFF by default)
  Reads the seller and shipper from the product page and shows them as a "Sold by / Ships from" badge at the top of the product info, green for items sold by Amazon and orange for marketplace sellers, so you can check who you're buying from at a glance.

・🔊 Volume Booster (OFF by default)
  Adjusts tab volume from 0% to 300%. It applies to the tab where you open the popup, and is automatically reapplied when you switch back to a tab that is already boosted. Works on any site.
  ・Auto Distortion Guard: tames sudden peaks to reduce clipping when boosting
  ・Night Mode: gently compresses loud voices and explosions so quiet sounds aren't buried
  ・Wall-Thump Guard: cuts deep bass that carries through walls and floors
  ・10-band equalizer: ±12 dB per band from 32 Hz to 16 kHz, with a preamp and 9 presets (switches to Custom when you adjust it yourself)
  ・Mute button: silence and restore in one click while keeping your settings
  These options also work at 100% volume. Audio processing is released as soon as it's no longer needed.

・🎞️ Video Gamma (OFF by default)
  Applies gamma correction to videos on the page. The slider's center (1.0) means no correction; move right to lift shadows and brighten, or left to add contrast and darken (range 0.3–3.0). One setting covers all tabs, including embedded videos.

・🖥️ Remove video black bars (OFF by default)
  Removes letterbox and pillarbox bars on ultrawide and other monitors by zooming (keeping the aspect ratio) or stretching the video to fill the screen. Pick your monitor's aspect ratio or resolution, and each video's shape is detected automatically.

・🔍 Loupe (OFF by default)
  A circular magnifier that follows your cursor. It magnifies a snapshot of the current view, so you can inspect the details of a paused video too. Choose 1.5x, 2.5x or 4x zoom and a lens diameter of 150–1000 px. Left-click the page to turn it off.

・🎨 Color Picker (always available)
  Pick any color on screen with the eyedropper and copy it to the clipboard as HEX, RGB or HSL. You can also paste a color code to add it. Up to 20 picked colors are kept as history on your device.

■ How to use

1. Click the toolbar icon to open the popup.
2. Choose the section you want from the menu at the top.
3. Use the switches, sliders and selectors in each section. Changes are saved automatically and take effect right away.

Volume Booster, Video Gamma, black-bar removal, Loupe and the three Amazon features are under Tune. The popup remembers the last section you opened.

■ Save and load settings
Under Settings you can sync settings across PCs through your browser's sync service and export or import them as a JSON file. Backups include all feature settings, the equalizer, blocked channels, color history and the selected section, and can be moved to another PC or between Chrome and Firefox. Sync on/off, the Gemini Notebook account, sign-in details, caches and on-page positions stay on each device. The interface is available in 9 languages (English, Japanese, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, Korean, Spanish, French, German and Brazilian Portuguese). It follows your browser's language, and you can also choose one next to the popup title. Light and dark themes are supported.

■ Privacy

・Settings are normally stored on your device. Only if you turn on settings sync are eligible settings shared through your browser's sync service. Game-screen zoom areas and the chosen display language stay on your device and are not synced.
・Page content and tab audio are not collected or sent for advertising, analytics or telemetry. Volume Booster only amplifies and plays back audio; nothing is recorded, saved or sent.
・Network access is limited to what the features you use or enable need:
  - Instagram / TikTok / X image saving: images are fetched from each service's image servers only when you click the save button (no cookies or referrer are sent)
  - YouTube Enhancements: only while hiding channels from other countries, showing all subscriptions, the subscriptions grid or broadcast time is on, related YouTube pages (channel about pages, the subscriptions list, watch pages) and thumbnail images are fetched
  - Send to Gemini Notebook: only when the sub-feature is on and you use the send panel, the extension talks to Gemini Notebook with your signed-in Google account to check the destination and add URLs such as videos
  - Amazon Subscribe & Save monthly total: only while it's on, prices are read from amazon.co.jp. Orders and deliveries are never changed, and results stay within the page
  - Settings sync: uses your browser's sync service only when turned on
  - Contact: what you enter is sent to Kagayoi Support only when you submit the form
・Browsing data is never sent to the developer's servers.
